About us · the crew · the warehouse
We will never take you somewhere we haven't been
Zaczęliśmy w 2008 roku od transportu motocykli. Najpierw dla siebie, potem dla kolegów, w końcu dla ludzi z całej Europy. Od kilku lat wozimy też samochody i quady. Pakujemy w dwóch magazynach — w Łowiczu przy ul. Małszyce 9 i w Ciechanowie. To tam maszyny trafiają do klatek i stamtąd jadą na odprawę.
The expeditions came later and grew out of the same thing: we knew the roads, the customs officers and the people along the way, so we started taking others with us. Kyrgyzstan, the Pamirs, Tibet, Pakistan, Afghanistan, Patagonia, Africa, Alaska. Every route has a support vehicle, a guide and someone who has been there before.
We don't sell dreams in a PDF. We state the difficulty on a x/10 scale, the price openly, and one rule we never bend: the quoted price is final.
„We simply know a thing or two about good adventures.”
